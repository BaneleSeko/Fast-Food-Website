# About Street Bites

## Good Food. Good Mood.

Welcome to **Street Bites**, where great taste meets the love of good food!

We are all about serving delicious, satisfying and affordable fast food that brings people together. Inspired by South African street-food culture, Street Bites brings you a variety of tasty meals prepared to make every bite enjoyable.

### Our Story

Street Bites was created around one simple idea: good food should be enjoyable, accessible and memorable.

From classic burgers and crispy chicken to loaded chips, tasty wraps and traditional South African amaGwinya, our menu brings together familiar favourites and delicious street-food experiences.

Whether you're grabbing a quick meal, sharing food with friends or treating yourself after a long day, Street Bites is here to make your food experience worthwhile.

### What We Offer

- **Burgers:** Delicious burgers packed with flavour.
- **Chicken:** Crispy, satisfying chicken meals.
- **Chips:** Golden, crunchy chips, including loaded options.
- **Wraps:** Tasty meals wrapped up for convenience.
- **AmaGwinya:** A South African favourite, soft and golden on the outside and fluffy on the inside.
- **Drinks:** Refreshing beverages to complete your meal.

### Our Mission

Our mission is to provide enjoyable, affordable and convenient food while creating a welcoming experience for every customer.

We believe that food is more than just a meal — it's about bringing people together and creating moments worth enjoying.

### Why Choose Street Bites?

- A variety of delicious fast-food options.
- Convenient online ordering.
- Simple and user-friendly shopping experience.
- Delivery and collection ordering options.
- A menu inspired by local food culture.

### Our Promise

Every customer deserves a satisfying food experience. We aim to make ordering easy, enjoyable and convenient from browsing our menu to receiving your order.
